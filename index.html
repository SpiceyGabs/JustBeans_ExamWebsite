About Us

Proudly South African ·Est 2001

Grounded in our love for authentic flavour,community, and convenience, we source our beans ethically from across the globe. From the lush, amazonian plantations of Brazil, to the picturesque highlands of Kenya and the rich soils of Rwanda.


Each bean is carefully roasted and packaged right here in South Africa, ensuring every shipment and cup supports local hands and sustainable farming.


Our goal? To bring you closer to the craft. From farm to roast, to cup. We invite you to explore, taste, and trace the journey of your coffee through our interactive maps and honest, transparent process.

Meet the Team!

JustBeans began with three South African university students; a diverse team of language and economics graduates from UCT who found themselves unemployed in 2001. Armed with a shared passion for coffee, culture, and community, they started selling freshly brewed coffee out of a small rented truck at campus events and on campus during the exam season.

What started as a side hustle has since evolved into a proudly local brand that blends heritage and innovation. Today, the team continues to break into both local and digital markets, delivering the same quality and convenience that fueled their humble beginnings.
